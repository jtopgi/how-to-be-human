# Changelog

## Version 1.1 · September 27, 2026

- Every chapter now opens with a real, documented story. Eight chapters have new openings: Jackie Robinson and Branch Rickey (5), James Stockdale (6), Anthony Trollope (11), Nicholas Winton (17), the Amish of Nickel Mines (18), Roseto, Pennsylvania (22), Marienthal, Austria (25), and Thomas Merton (36).
- Nothing is repeated. Each story, study, quotation, and explanation now appears in one place only. The Practice's "Roots" lines no longer retell the chapters, and chapters no longer restate the rituals' steps.
- Cross-references like "(see #12)" and ritual codes like "(H4)" are now tappable links.
- Removed a leftover editing note in chapter 16, and corrected a few citation details.
