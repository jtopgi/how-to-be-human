# How to Be Human

*The guidelines humanity agrees on, and how to practice them*  
Compiled and edited by Jeevan Topgi

**Read it free:** https://jtopgi.github.io/how-to-be-human/

[Download the EPUB](How-to-Be-Human.epub) (447 KB, for Kindle and e-readers) · [Download the PDF](How-to-Be-Human.pdf) (5.5 MB, 6×9 book layout)

No one is born with a manual for being human. This free book keeps only the advice that science, philosophy, the world's religions, societies across history, and people near the end of life agree on: 36 guidelines, each with its own chapter, and 44 rituals to practice them. Every factual claim is sourced.

## The 36 guidelines

**Body**

1. Move every day.
2. Sleep enough, and keep a day of rest.
3. Eat simply, drink little, and let nothing own you.

**Mind**

4. Pay attention to the life in front of you.
5. Feel your feelings; choose your actions.
6. Work on what you can control; release the rest.
7. Hold your beliefs, and yourself, humbly.
8. Stay curious; keep learning.

**Character**

9. Examine your life, and live by what you find.
10. Tell the truth, kindly, and keep your word.
11. Build the habits of the person you want to become.
12. Be brave when it matters.
13. Seek the middle way.

**Relationships**

14. Put people first.
15. Listen more than you speak.
16. Treat love as a practice, not just a feeling.
17. Care for the young; honor the old.
18. Forgive, and make amends.

**Society**

19. See the dignity in every person.
20. Treat others as you would want to be treated.
21. Be generous.
22. Belong to a community, and show up for it.
23. Welcome the stranger.
24. Stand up for fairness, and use power to serve.

**Work & Money**

25. Do useful work, and do it well.
26. Know how much is enough.
27. Spend less than you earn; save for lean years.

**Joy**

28. Give thanks every day.
29. Make room for play and beauty.
30. Spend time in nature, protect it, and be kind to its creatures.

**Hardship**

31. Expect suffering, and don't carry it alone.
32. Grieve fully; love continues.
33. When you fail, forgive yourself and begin again.

**Mortality & Meaning**

34. Remember that you will die.
35. Live for something larger than yourself.
36. Make time for awe and stillness.

## How this book was made

Each guideline had to be supported by at least four of five independent lenses (science, philosophy, religion, society, and the testimony of elders), contradicted by none, and taught by at least three religious traditions. Questions that failed that test, such as whether there is a God or what happens after death, are set out in their own section instead of being answered.

This book was conceived, compiled, and edited by Jeevan Topgi, who set its question, its agreement rule, and its standards of evidence. The text was drafted with AI tools (GitHub Copilot), and its claims were checked against the sources in the Notes. The wisdom itself belongs to the traditions, thinkers, and researchers it cites.
